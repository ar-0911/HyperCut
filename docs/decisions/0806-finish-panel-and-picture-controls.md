# 0806: Finish panel, on-screen picker and picture controls

Status: accepted, 2026-10-20

**Decision.**
- Under the preview, "On screen" lists the graphics at the playhead; clicking one selects it, so a graphic can be
  picked from the picture as well as from the timeline. The Inspector edits its words and numbers (as before) and
  now its colour.
- Stock and generated pictures show their search words or prompt with "Try another" / "Fetch now", their credit
  and, for stills, the movement. A motion moment shows whether it plays a generated clip.
- The toolbar shows "Fetch N pictures" when the plan waits for some, and what this video has spent. Pictures the
  AI box adds are fetched right away; fetched graphics are merged into the plan as it is then, so edits made
  meanwhile stay.
- A Finish panel: check the render (review, 0019, with "Apply fixes" as one undo), make variants (links to the new
  jobs), save the style as a recipe, export for an editor (0017). The upload page lists recipes.
- The progress list shows "fetch pictures" only while it runs.
