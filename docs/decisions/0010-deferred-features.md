# 0010: Deliberately not in the MVP

Status: accepted, 2026-10-17

These are planned but left out of the first version, each for a stated reason:

- **Animations on and around the speaker while they talk** (Adi's request): needs per-frame person
  segmentation to layer graphics behind or around the speaker. Planned right after the MVP.
- **Text behind the subject**: same segmentation work as above.
- **AI b-roll and stock cutaways**: generated shots must be separate cutaways, never over the speaker (ADR 0001).
- **Music and sound effects**: needs a licensed library and ducking under speech.
- **Long-form clipping** into several shorts: needs chunked analysis (ADR 0002).
- **Multi-speaker reframing**: needs a tracker that switches between people (ADR 0007).
- **Timeline editor, brand kits, direct publishing to TikTok, Reels and Shorts**: product features once the
  core output is good.
